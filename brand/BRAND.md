# Dark Tunnel brand

Generated by `node scripts/brand.mjs` (the wordmark outline comes from `scripts/outline-wordmark.py`). Edit the generator, not the files. `brand-sheet.png` shows everything on one page.

## What it is

A tunnel from the dark web to web3. Open any dapp over Tor with a throwaway ghost wallet. The app you open sees the tunnel, not your connection.

**Line:** A tunnel from the dark web to web3.
**Short:** Open any dapp over Tor with a throwaway ghost wallet.
**Support line:** Apps see the tunnel, not your connection.
**Meta description:** Dark Tunnel opens web3 apps for you from the dark web, over Tor, and signs with a throwaway ghost wallet. Apps see the tunnel, not your connection.
**Two sentences:** Dark Tunnel is a browser window for web3 that works from the dark web. Paste any app address and it opens through the tunnel, while a fresh ghost wallet made in your browser signs for you.

## Mark

A file with the marker struck across it. The product decides what an app can learn about you, and the bar is the strike. One shape, two colours.

- Keep the bar. Never outline the mark, tilt it, round it, or add a gradient or glow.
- Clear space on every side: the height of the bar.
- Smallest size: 16 px on screen.
- Files: `mark/mark-bone.svg` (on dark), `mark/mark-ground.svg` (on light), `mark/mark-on-ground.svg`, `mark/mark-on-bone.svg`.

## Wordmark and lockups

`dark tunnel`, always lowercase, Archivo condensed (width 62) extra bold, outlined to paths. In a lockup the mark is as tall as the tall letters, with a gap of about 0.4 of that height.

- `wordmark/lockup-bone.svg` (on dark), `lockup-ground.svg` (on light), `lockup-stacked-bone.svg`, `wordmark-bone.svg`, `wordmark-ground.svg`.
- Do not set the name in another typeface, in capitals, or as two words in different weights.

## Type

- **Archivo**, condensed 800: headlines and the wordmark. Sentence case, tight leading (0.9).
- **Archivo**, normal width: text.
- **Martian Mono**: addresses, labels, logs.

## The texture

Faint traffic-log lines (documentation addresses only, never real ones) with a few tokens struck out in bone bars. Used on social images and the site's background. The bars are the brand's idea in one picture.

## Voice

Plain and specific. Short sentences. Say what the tunnel does and nothing past it.

- No "anonymous", "untraceable" or "unhackable". Say "your IP stays out of it".
- No em dashes. No hype words (revolutionary, seamless, next-gen).
- Never imply the operator cannot see traffic: it can. Say so where it matters.
- Money words stay literal: ETH, not "value".
